## Pillars

Capture:
Android field capture for farmer, parcel, crop, activity, media, and field event evidence.

Coordinate:
FPO/company/project operations, farmer cohorts, field-agent workflows, and admin visibility.

Sync:
Offline-first queue durability, replay, idempotency, conflict recovery, and backlog draining.

Advise:
Broadcasts, advisory delivery, media attachments, language fallback, audience targeting, and read/ack analytics.

Govern:
Backend-owned contracts, labels, options, localization overrides, land-intelligence summaries, and audit.

Extend:
Roadmap foundation for insurance, subsidy, credit, weather, soil, satellite, and risk review.

## Claim boundaries

- Capture, Coordinate, Sync, Advise, and Govern are implemented foundation.
- Extend is foundation/roadmap and must not imply live risk scoring or automated claim decisions.
- Icons should be simple enough for landing cards and video overlays.

## Visual acceptance checklist

- Icons are visually consistent with existing dark SVG palette.
- Individual icons are readable at small size.
- Combined grid has no overlap.
- Extend uses dashed/amber roadmap styling.
